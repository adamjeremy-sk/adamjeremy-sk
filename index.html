I'm Adam Jeremy, an independent developer in Saskatoon who builds software for the satisfaction of building it, and who tends to choose projects where privacy and local control are part of the design rather than an afterthought. My current work includes an Android weather and astronomy application that operates entirely without location permissions, and a native Linux code editor with an integrated AI agent that can run on local hardware. Everything I publish is released under the GNU General Public License, because I believe people should be able to examine, verify and modify the software they depend on every day.
